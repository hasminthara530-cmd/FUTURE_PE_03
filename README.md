#Task 3: AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator


##1. Business Profile:

Business Type: Digital Marketing Agency

Business Name: Growth Spark Digtal 

Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu

Goal:

To generate organic traffic from Google and convert visitors into leads using SEO-optimized blog content.

Target Audience:

Small business owners, startups, and local brands looking for digital marketing services in Chennai.

##2. Structured Prompt Design

Master Prompt

Act as an SEO content strategist and blog writer for a digital marketing agency named Growth Spark Digital located in Chennai.

1. Generate a pillar blog:
   
> - Target a primary keyword related to digital marketing services in Chennai
  
> - Create a detailed blog with H1, H2, H3 headings
  
> - Include introduction, detailed sections, and conclusion
> - Add internal linking suggestions
> - Keep content informative and SEO-optimized

2. Generate 3–5 supporting blogs:
   
> - Each blog should target a related keyword

> - Include SEO-friendly headings (H1–H3)

> - Focus on specific topics like cost, services, or how-to guides

3. Include:
   
> - Keyword strategy (primary + secondary keywords)
  
> - Search intent (informational / transactional)
  
> - Local SEO focus (Chennai-based keywords)

Keep the tone professional, clear, and helpful. Avoid keyword stuffing.
